System Details

  • Stage 1 (Localization): YOLOv8 model trained as a 1-class detector to locate sign bounding boxes.
  • Stage 2 (Classification): Custom VGG-style CNN trained on the GTSRB dataset to classify cropped signs into 43 categories.
  • Filtering: Detections with CNN prediction confidence below 80% are automatically rejected.
